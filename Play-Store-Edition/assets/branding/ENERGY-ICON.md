## Prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Design a bold energetic Android launcher icon for FocusBro, a productivity app that locks distractions and protects focus time. Completely new direction: electric cobalt blue opaque full-bleed square background, vivid acid-lime chunky padlock silhouette in the center, with a crisp lightning-bolt negative-space cutout through the lock body. The lock shackle should be clearly recognizable, rounded and thick. Surround the lock with four short clean ivory focus-corner brackets, forming a tight visual focus frame, all visually unified as one powerful emblem. Dynamic athletic-tech energy, confident and motivating, excellent small-size readability. Flat, immaculate vector-like graphic, precise edges, strong geometric shapes, subtle forward momentum in the bolt. All essential shapes including brackets fit within the centered 60% of the square, safe for circular Android launcher masking. One icon only. Opaque color everywhere. No leaf, plant, timer ring, text, letters, gradients, shadows, texture, 3D, watermarks, rounded outer tile, mockup, or multiple proposals. Production app icon alone.
